# Voltura Reel Player

Watch Facebook Reels, photo and video Stories, and YouTube videos and Shorts fullscreen in desktop Chrome. Step through a video, repeat a section, or zoom in on a detail.

**[Add to Chrome for free](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/voltura-reel-player/lmifcnkdamfogmmenenndpccgcbjnedn?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=reel_player_launch&utm_content=readme)** · [Watch the Facebook demo](https://reels.voltura.se/#demo) · [Privacy](https://reels.voltura.se/privacy.html)

[![Voltura Reel Player fullscreen preview](https://reels.voltura.se/media/social-preview.jpg)](https://reels.voltura.se/)

## What you can do

- Watch Facebook Reels, photo or video Stories, and YouTube videos and Shorts fullscreen. Navigation and seek buttons stay in fixed positions as the content changes.
- Scrub a video, step by 0.1 seconds, jump by 5 seconds with Shift and the arrow keys, or play at 0.10× to 4× speed.
- Repeat a video or select a segment with range handles or A/B marks. Toggle spoken captions when the video offers them.
- Zoom up to 10×, pan, and save a paused video frame as a PNG or the displayed Story image.
- Hide Reel overlays and choose from 10 themes and 16 languages. Set the control bar width and whether it fades when idle.

## Using the player

### Facebook

Open a Facebook reel or Story and select the **Reel Player icon** or press **F**. Move the pointer to reveal the controls. Hover over a button to see its keyboard shortcuts. Press **F** again or press **Esc** to leave fullscreen.

The Story viewer keeps Facebook’s progress bars. Use **‹** and **›** to move between cards or **|‹** and **›|** to switch Stories. Switching Stories starts on the first card. A paused Story stays paused when you navigate.

If a card links to a Reel, select **Play video** or click the picture. The Reel plays in fullscreen and inherits the Story’s mute state. Select **Back to Story** to return. When Facebook offers them, the right-side stack shows its seven reactions and **Share**, which opens Facebook’s sharing panel.

Pause on the image you want, then click **Save image** or press **S**. This saves a video frame as PNG, downloads the displayed Story photo, or crops a linked Reel preview to its inner picture. The player confirms when the file is saved.

### YouTube videos and Shorts

YouTube support requires **1.2.0 or later**. Open Voltura Reel Player from the Chrome toolbar, select **Enable YouTube**, and approve YouTube access once. Open a video or Short and select the **Voltura Reel Player icon** beside the player or press **F**. Press **F** again or **Esc** to leave Voltura Reel Player fullscreen. Use YouTube’s own fullscreen button to enter its fullscreen viewer.

Seek, adjust speed, loop a segment, zoom, or move between Shorts in fullscreen. Captions, paused-frame saving, Like, Dislike, Comments, Share, and Subscribe are available when YouTube offers them. Comments, Share, and Subscribe return to the YouTube page. You can turn off YouTube access from the same popup.

### Playback preferences

To repeat a segment, drag the range handles or Shift-drag on the timeline, then turn on Repeat. Open the extension from Chrome’s toolbar to change your preferences.
