Voltura Reel Player

Privacy

Voltura Reel Player processes playback information in Chrome to display fullscreen controls. Voltura AB receives no video or Story content, browsing history, account information, or telemetry from the extension.

Facebook Reels and Stories

The extension reads the current reel’s video state, author name and photo, description, and available controls on Facebook. In Stories, it reads the current video state or displayed image and the available playback and reaction controls. Facebook handles liking, reacting, sharing, following, and saving to Facebook through its own controls. Linked Reels play in Facebook’s embedded player. Author photos are loaded from their original page URLs.

YouTube Shorts

YouTube access is optional. Chrome grants access to www.youtube.com when you enable Shorts in the extension popup. The extension checks the page address to recognize when you open a Short. On a Short it reads playback state, displayed creator and caption details, and available controls. YouTube handles likes, comments, sharing, and subscriptions through its own interface. Turn off Shorts in the popup to revoke access.

Preferences and downloads

Chrome stores your player preferences locally. When you choose Save image, the extension uses Chrome’s Downloads API to save the selected frame, photo, or cropped linked Reel preview to your device and check whether that download completed. To crop a linked Reel preview, it retrieves the selected image from Facebook’s image servers and crops it locally. Removing the extension clears its stored preferences. Downloaded files stay on your device until you delete them.
