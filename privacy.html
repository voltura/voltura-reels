Voltura Reel Player

Privacy

Voltura Reel Player processes playback information in Chrome to display fullscreen controls. Voltura AB receives no Reel or Story content, browsing history, account information, or telemetry from the extension.

Facebook Reels and video Stories

The extension reads the current reel’s video state, author name and photo, description, and available controls on Facebook. For video Stories, it reads the current video state and playback controls. Actions such as liking, following, or saving use Facebook’s controls and are handled by Facebook. Author photos are loaded from their original page URLs.

Preferences and saved frames

Chrome stores your player preferences locally. When you choose to save a frame, Chrome downloads the PNG to your device. Removing the extension clears its stored preferences. Downloaded files stay on your device until you delete them.
